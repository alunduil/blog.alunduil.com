# Orconomics — review spine

J. Zachary Pike, *Orconomics* (2014), book one of the Dark Profit Saga.

**Thesis:** Read it, then go straight on to *Son of a Liche*. It's a
well-executed satire of capitalism running amok.

## Supporting points (thematic, not chronological)

1. Every fantasy convention has a financial counterpart. Evidence: a
   monster's hoard is claimed and sold to plunder funds before any hero has
   killed it. Firms like Goldson Baggs run the funds. The Heroes' Guild
   licenses heroes and ranks them by points. Goblins need papers to live
   among everyone else.
2. Gorm is honour in the gutter. Evidence: he has lost his licence and his
   clan and robs adventurers for a living, yet he sticks up for an
   undocumented goblin. He stays loyal to his friends when it costs him.

## Honest tail

I read it as the 2008 housing crisis. Pike wrote it after following
coverage of the crash
(<https://fantasy-hive.co.uk/2023/09/interview-with-j-zachary-pike-the-dark-profit-saga/>).
Whether it stays true beyond 2008 is open.
